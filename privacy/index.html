Privacy

What we collect, and what we don't.

Plain language, because you shouldn't need a lawyer to understand what happens to your information.

The short version

We collect an email address only if you type one into our signup form. We use privacy-first website analytics that set no cookies and cannot identify you. We never see your payment information. We do not sell or share your data with anyone.

If you sign up for updates

We collect your email address and nothing else. We use it to send occasional updates about our programs. Every message includes an unsubscribe link, and you can also email us at hello@fgkidsproject.com to be removed at any time.

Signups are handled by Netlify, the service that hosts this website. Your address is stored in our account there and exported only into tools we use to send our own updates.

If you donate

Donations are processed by Zeffy, a fundraising platform for nonprofits. Payment details go directly to Zeffy and their payment processor — they never pass through this website, and we never see or store your card number.

We do receive the information Zeffy shares with us as the recipient organization, which typically includes your name, email address, donation amount, and date, so we can thank you, issue a receipt, and keep the records a 501(c)(3) is required to keep. Zeffy's own handling of your data is governed by their privacy policy.

Website analytics

We use Cloudflare Web Analytics to understand which pages people find useful. It is cookieless and privacy-first: it does not track you across websites, does not build a profile of you, and does not collect information that identifies you personally. We see aggregate numbers — page views, referring sites, general country — and nothing tied to an individual.

Because we set no tracking cookies, there is no cookie banner on this site. That is deliberate.

Children's privacy

Our programs serve young people, but this website is written for adults — parents, educators, donors, and partners. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 13. If you believe a child has submitted information to us, contact us and we will delete it promptly.

Who we share information with

We do not sell, rent, or trade your information. We share it only with the service providers named above who make this website and our donation process work, and only to the extent they need it. We may disclose information if required by law.

Your choices

You can unsubscribe from emails at any time. You can ask us what information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it — email hello@fgkidsproject.com and we will respond.

Changes

If we change how we handle your information, we will update this page and change the date below.
